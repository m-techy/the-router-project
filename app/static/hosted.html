WHY ROUTING MATTERS

Free APIs become useful when failure is expected, not exceptional.

One provider can throttle, rename a model, or change a free tier. Your app should not need to care.

A provider hits 429.

The model cools down; the router continues down the ranked candidate list.

A compatibility facade rejects a feature.

Gemini can fall back from its OpenAI-compatible surface to the native REST API.

An upstream catalog changes.

The dashboard surfaces the diff for review instead of silently changing your routes.

What the gateway should handle so your app does not.
